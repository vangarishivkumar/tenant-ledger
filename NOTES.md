- **Chosen Problems**: Solved partial-success bulk ingestion, DB-enforced idempotency, strict tenant isolation, immutable append-only reversals, point-in-time balances, audit trails, and support redaction because financial correctness and auditability are non-negotiable.
- **Data Integrity**: Used `Numeric(18,4)` instead of floats, nested savepoints per row so bad records never block valid ones, and unique constraints on `(tenant_id, idempotency_key)` and `reverses_entry_id` to prevent race-condition double-credits or double-reversals.
- **Skipped**: Real JWT/OAuth identity provider (simulated via `X-Tenant-ID`/`X-Role` headers), per-tenant rate limiting, and CSV parsing (assumed JSON ingestion layer) to keep scope tight and rock-solid within 60 minutes.
- **What Breaks at 100x Load**: On-the-fly `SUM()` queries over `ledger_entries` for large clients will degrade; at 100x scale, migrate from SQLite to PostgreSQL (using Row-Level Security), partition `ledger_entries` by `tenant_id`/`effective_at`, and maintain pre-aggregated monthly/daily balance snapshots.
